Soil Depletion Trajectories in Sand:

• Nitrogen (Urea): 6-week horizon [100%, 85%, 70%, 50%, 25%, 10%, 0%]. Rapid leaching as NO₃⁻.

• Phosphorus (SSP): 12-week horizon [100%, 96%, 92%, 86%, 80%, 74%, 68%, 58%, 48%, 35%, 22%, 10%, 0%].

• Potassium (MOP): 10-week horizon [100%, 92%, 84%, 74%, 62%, 52%, 42%, 30%, 20%, 10%, 0%].

• Calcium (SSP + Gypsum): 20-week horizon. Divalent cation with moderate retention.

• Magnesium (MgSO₄): 16-week horizon. High solubility in sand; displaced by Al/Ca.

• Sulfur (SSP + Gypsum + MgSO₄): 7-week horizon. Rapid leaching as sulfate anion.

• Boron (Borax): 6-week horizon. Moves freely as uncharged boric acid.

• Zinc (Zn-EDTA): 18-week horizon. Chelate ring prevents fixation, providing steady root availability.
