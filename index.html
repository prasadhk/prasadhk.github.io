Agricultural Reference & Secondary Nutrient Role

Granulated Dolomite (19Ca:11Mg)

Standard 25kg bag. Premium natural soil conditioner providing 19% Calcium and 11% Magnesium. Neutralizes acidic soils (common in high-rainfall Western Ghats) while preventing Mg chlorosis.

SPIC Keerthi CMS (15Ca:3Mg:5S)

Standard 50kg bag. Balanced multi-secondary granule delivering 15% Calcium, 3% Magnesium, and 5% Sulfur. Perfect for Arecanut and plantation crops needing continuous root calcium.

Urea (45kg) & MOP (50kg)

Standardized commercial Indian packaging. Urea provides 46% N in 45kg sacks, and MOP supplies 60% K₂O in 50kg bags for nut/fruit filling and drought tolerance.

DAP & Ammonium Sulfate

DAP supplies 18% N and 46% P₂O₅ for initial root development. Ammonium Sulfate (20.5% N, 24% S) delivers high-efficiency ammoniacal nitrogen with sulfur acidification.
